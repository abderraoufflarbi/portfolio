01Profile

Not simply completing tasks, but building workflows that last.

My approach combines operational execution with process development: structured, traceable and repeatable ways of working that improve how an organisation runs.

I joined FECO in October 2025 on the commercial side. Purchasing became part of my work in March 2026, and in May 2026 I moved formally into General Services and Procurement. That path took me from client-facing work into the operational backbone of the company, and it gives me a view of both sides of a purchase: the commercial environment suppliers work in, and the discipline needed to run the buying well.

Today I work across procurement, supplier management, financial coordination, asset traceability and administrative processes. What interests me most is connecting purchasing, finance, inventory and operational needs into one coherent system.
