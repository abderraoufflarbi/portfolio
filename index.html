About

That move meant taking over procurement, stock and administration at once, with no formal handover. I learned each procedure by doing it and documented it as I went.

The purchase-to-payment workflow

Introduced on 18 August 2026 so that every purchase is traceable from need to payment.

  1. 01Requirement
  2. 02Quotation
  3. 03Purchase order
  4. 04Reception
  5. 05Invoice
  6. 06Payment
  7. 07Archiving
  1. 01

    Requirement

    Every purchase starts from a defined need.

  2. 02

    Quotation

    Suppliers are searched and quotations compared.

  3. 03

    Purchase order

    The Bon de Commande formalises the choice.

  4. 04

    Reception

    Goods are received against the order.

  5. 05

    Invoice

    The supplier invoice joins the purchase file.

  6. 06

    Payment

    The purchase is paid. Four have gone through the bank so far.

  7. 07

    Archiving

    The complete file is archived, so the purchase can be traced end to end.
