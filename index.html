01Professional profile

Not simply completing tasks, but building workflows that last.

My approach combines operational execution with process development: structured, traceable and repeatable workflows that improve organizational efficiency.

My professional journey at FECO began in October 2025 as a Chargé Commercial (Sales Executive). I started handling purchasing activities in March 2026, and the formal transition to General Services and Procurement (MGX) took place in May 2026. This expanded my responsibilities from commercial activities into the operational backbone of the company.

Today, my work focuses on procurement operations, supplier management, financial coordination, asset traceability, administrative processes and operational improvement. My background in commercial operations also gives me an understanding of supplier relationships, business development and the commercial environment in which procurement decisions operate.

I am particularly interested in developing procurement systems that connect purchasing, finance, inventory and operational requirements into one coherent process. My direction: Procurement and Supply Chain Management, with a focus on international procurement, operational excellence and digital transformation.

02Featured work

The purchase-to-payment workflow

A purchase-to-payment workflow, in use since 18 August 2026, so that purchasing stays documented and traceable from the initial requirement to final archiving. Scroll to move through the stages.

  1. 01

    Requirement

    Every purchase starts from a defined need.

    Record kept Why the purchase is needed.

  2. 02

    Supplier quotation & comparison

    Suppliers are sourced and quotations compared.

    Record kept Which suppliers were consulted and how their offers compare.

  3. 03

    Purchase order

    The Bon de commande formalises the choice.

    Record kept The decision, formalised as a purchase order.

  4. 04

    Reception

    Goods are received against the order.

    Record kept That the goods arrived and correspond to the order.

  5. 05

    Invoice

    The supplier invoice joins the purchase file.

    Record kept The supplier’s invoice, added to the same file.

  6. 06

    Payment

    Payment is coordinated and documented.

    Record kept The settlement of the purchase.

  7. 07

    Archiving

    The complete file is archived, so the purchase can be traced end to end.

    Record kept The full file, from need to payment.

Purpose
Keep purchasing activities documented and traceable from the initial requirement to final archiving.
Connects
Requirements, quotations, purchase orders, reception, invoices, payments and archiving.
Alongside
Expense-tracking documentation that improves spending visibility.
Status
In use since 18 August 2026.
